A switch for your microphone.

Help with setup, supported devices, and microphone settings.

Need help?

Open a support request or browse existing issues. Include your macOS version and microphone model. Support issues are public, so leave out passwords, audio recordings, and personal information.

Getting started

Open Husky Hush. It starts in mute mode. Use the microphone item in the menu bar to open the controls, then select “Turn microphones on” or “Mute microphones.” Close the controls window to continue using it from the menu bar.

“Check mic” or “Check your microphones”

At least one input could not be controlled or its mute state could not be verified. Check the input-device list. Do not assume an unsupported device is muted.

Supported microphones

The app requires a writable hardware input mute control or writable input-volume controls for all channels. The built-in MacBook Pro microphone has been tested with its hardware mute control. Some USB, Bluetooth, virtual, and iPhone/Continuity microphones expose no compatible control. For example, an attached iPhone microphone was identified as unsupported during testing. Choose a supported microphone in your meeting app when using Husky Hush.

Other apps still show microphone access

This is expected: input muting does not revoke microphone permission. Apps can retain permission while the supported device’s input is muted. Husky Hush is an input-control utility, not a system privacy or security boundary.

My mic stayed muted after quitting

Reopen Husky Hush and select “Turn microphones on.” You may also check System Settings → Sound → Input. Quitting intentionally does not turn a microphone on.

Device changes and waking your Mac

Waking your Mac or discovering a newly attached input returns the app to mute mode. Input control changes are verified periodically. Audio may briefly pass while a device changes or between checks.

Optional supporter purchase

Click the FREE label to see the optional purchase. Pay only if you like the app and want to support its developer. The only benefit is changing FREE to PAID; all microphone controls work identically without paying. This is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with Restore Purchase support. A refunded or revoked purchase returns the label to FREE. Apple handles payment and displays the local price.

Open at login

Enable “Open at login” in Husky Hush. If macOS requests approval, review it in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions.

Price and system requirements

Every Husky Hush feature is free. An optional one-time $0.99 USD supporter purchase only changes the FREE label to PAID. It unlocks no features and is never required. There are no subscriptions; the App Store shows the local price before purchase. It requires macOS 14 or later. App Store availability will be announced after submission and Apple review.
