Free macOS utility

Your microphone.
Your switch.

Keep supported microphones muted until you explicitly turn them on, with a clear control in your Mac’s menu bar.

Know what’s muted.

See live input-control values, restore previous settings, and get a clear warning for microphones the app cannot control.

  • Starts in mute mode.
  • Returns to mute mode on wake or when a new input is discovered.
  • Optional launch at login.
  • No accounts, subscriptions, ads, analytics, or audio recording. An optional $0.99 USD supporter purchase only changes the FREE label to PAID; every feature remains free.
